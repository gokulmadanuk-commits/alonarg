App icons
=========

This folder must contain two PNG icons that the rest of the app references:

  icon-192.png   192 x 192 px
  icon-512.png   512 x 512 px

These are GENERATED BY THE ORCHESTRATOR and are intentionally NOT committed by
the PWA build step. The app (index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js) only
references them by path — do not block on their presence while developing.

Both are declared in manifest.webmanifest with purpose "any maskable", so they
should include a safe zone (keep important content within the central ~80% so
nothing critical is clipped when the platform applies a maskable shape).

The service worker precaches these paths but does so resiliently
(Promise.allSettled), so a missing icon during first deploy will not break
installation; once the icons exist they are cached on the next load.
